What PlantMe! does

PlantMe! suggests what to plant based on a short quiz, your ZIP code and the time of year, and shows planting dates. It is for home gardeners in the United States.

Planting dates are estimates

Dates are estimated from your ZIP code, typical frost dates and climate averages. Weather and local conditions vary, especially in hills, mountains and deserts. Check your local conditions before you plant. We cannot promise that any plant will grow, produce or survive. Any frost information PlantMe! shows comes from a forecast that is read only while the app is open. It can be wrong, and it is not a warning service, so do not rely on PlantMe! to tell you when a freeze is coming.
