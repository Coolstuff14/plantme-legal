Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026. This policy is from Jacob Lee ("we", "us"), who make the PlantMe! Garden Planner app ("PlantMe!").

The short version

  • No accounts. You never sign up or sign in.
  • Your gardens, quiz answers and saved plants stay on your phone.
  • No analytics SDK. No ads. We do not sell your data.
  • If you have Premium, the app asks the US National Weather Service for a forecast for the area of your ZIP code.
  • Purchases are handled by the store and by RevenueCat.

What stays on your device

Your quiz answers, gardens, ZIP code, saved plants, task progress and settings are stored in a database on your phone. We do not receive them and cannot see them. Reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled on your phone and do not use a server.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Subscriptions are sold by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat, a subscription service, to check whether you have Premium. RevenueCat creates a random anonymous ID for the app on your device and receives your purchase and subscription information from the store, such as which product you bought, when it renews, and the store receipt. It also receives basic device and app details, such as your device type, operating system, app version and IP address, as needed to run the service. We do not give RevenueCat your name, email, gardens or location, and we do not have your payment card details; the store keeps those. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Weather forecast: your ZIP area

With Premium, when you open the app, PlantMe! can check the forecast for frost. To do that it sends the approximate coordinates of your garden's ZIP code area (taken from a table inside the app, not your exact location) to the US National Weather Service at api.weather.gov. Like any internet request, this also shares your IP address with that service. No name or other ID is sent. This happens only while the app is open, and at most about once every six hours for each garden area. The Weather Service's own policy is at weather.gov/privacy.

Location permission

PlantMe! can ask for your phone's location permission, only if you tap the button to fill in your ZIP automatically. It is optional: you can always type your ZIP code instead. The request is for approximate (coarse) location. It is used once, to find a postal code, and that postal code is kept on your device like your other garden details. To look up the postal code, your phone sends the approximate coordinates to its maker's geocoding service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android), which has its own privacy terms. We never receive them. We do not store, upload or track your location. You can change the permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created on your phone. If you decline the permission, the app still works, without reminders. We do not use push notification servers.

What we do not do

  • We have no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • We do not include analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

Children

PlantMe! is a general gardening app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we have no accounts and keep your data on your device, we hold almost none, but if you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will look into it.

Deleting your data

Your garden data is on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. For purchase records held by RevenueCat or the store, contact us at the email below, and we will ask RevenueCat to delete what it holds for your anonymous ID, to the extent the law and the store's records allow. We may need your anonymous ID to find it; Apple or Google may keep their own purchase records.

Where this applies

PlantMe! is meant for people in the United States. Garden dates and the forecast cover the US only. If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date above.
